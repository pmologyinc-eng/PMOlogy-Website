About PMOlogy

Project-delivery expertise. Practical AI. Combined.

PMOlogy combines expertise in project management, project controls, delivery, and artificial intelligence to help organizations improve how projects are planned, monitored, controlled, and delivered.

Many organizations already have project management tools and established delivery processes, but those systems are often fragmented, manual, reactive, or underused. PMOlogy helps organizations improve visibility, decision-making, reporting, and delivery performance by adding an intelligence layer to existing project-control processes — introducing practical AI, automation, and data-driven capabilities into existing tools and workflows, without replacing current systems, redesigning the entire operating model, or committing to disproportionately large technology investments.

What makes PMOlogy different

Two domains, one team

Delivery expertise, not just technology

PMOlogy is built by a team with expertise across project delivery and artificial intelligence — understanding both the operational realities of project environments and the technical possibilities of AI, automation, and analytics.

Improve before replacing

PMOlogy looks first for ways to strengthen existing systems, processes, and tools before recommending major replacements — targeted, proportionate change that fits the environment already in place.

Real project-delivery needs, not generic AI

Unlike a general AI or automation agency, PMOlogy connects AI opportunities directly to the operational context of PMOs, engineering teams, project controls, and delivery governance.

Who's behind PMOlogy

Founding team

Mina Arzaghi, B.Eng., M.Sc., Ph.D.(c)

Co-Founder & CEO

Mina's path to PMOlogy runs through both project delivery and artificial intelligence — not one or the other. She began her career in project planning and cost control on engineering and construction projects, then moved into data science and machine learning in Canada, where she has spent the past decade teaching, researching, and applying AI at an academic and industry level most PMO leaders never get exposed to.

She holds a Bachelor's in Industrial Engineering, an M.Sc. in Business Intelligence, and a Project Management certificate from HEC Montréal, and is a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence through dual research at Mila – Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute and HEC Montréal, focused on trustworthy machine learning. For over ten years, she has also taught machine learning and data science at HEC Montréal to PhD, M.Sc., and MBA students.

Her industry experience spans data science and advisory roles at Desjardins and National Bank of Canada — including applied work on hyperparameter tuning through Bayesian optimization — alongside a research internship at Mila and early-career project controls work. As PMOlogy's Co-Founder & CEO, she leads the translation of that research-grade AI expertise into practical, responsible adoption for PMOs and capital program delivery — grounded in evidence, not hype.

Core strengths: project controls, trustworthy & applied machine learning, data science strategy, and AI adoption for PMOs and capital program delivery.

Javad Ramiani, B.Eng., M.P.M., PMP

Co-Founder & CTO

Javad's approach to project controls is grounded in the field, not the classroom. Trained as an Industrial Engineer and shaped by over 20 years inside engineering consulting and the construction of infrastructure, mining, and oil & energy projects, he has supported project teams at major engineering consultancy companies across Canada and the United States. That experience gives him a first-hand understanding of how capital programs actually behave under real technical and operational pressure — insight a purely administrative project management background doesn't provide.

That engineering foundation, paired with a Master's in Project Management and PMP certification, shapes his data-centric approach: building integrated master schedules, performance baselines, and forensic schedule analysis that hold up because they're rooted in how the work is actually engineered and executed, not just how it's reported.

Javad works closely with project and program delivery teams to build robust project controls frameworks — from baseline creation and progress tracking to real-time reporting pipelines that give leadership clear, defensible visibility into program performance. As PMOlogy's Co-Founder & CTO, he also leads the practical application of AI within PMO and project controls environments, extending that same disciplined, data-driven approach to how organizations adopt AI in their delivery operations.

Core strengths: project and program controls stand-up, forensic delay analysis, process automation, and AI adoption for PMOs and capital program delivery.
