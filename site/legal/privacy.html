Contact details (name, email, organization) when you fill out a form or contact us; website usage data (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages visited) via analytics tools, once in use; any other information you voluntarily provide.

To respond to your inquiries; to provide our services and support; to improve website functionality and performance; to comply with legal obligations.
