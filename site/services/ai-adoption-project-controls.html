AI Adoption in Project Controls

Adding an intelligence layer to project delivery, and uncovering hidden improvement potential — through a staged, responsible path to AI adoption.

AI is introduced thoughtfully, securely, and proportionately — supporting people and improving decisions without unnecessary complexity or risk. PMOlogy's approach moves through eight stages, from first assessing readiness through to scaling what actually works.

01

AI readiness assessment

Reviewing the processes, tools, and data already in place across the organization — the same improve-before-replacing discipline applied to AI specifically, to understand realistically where it can add value and where it can't.

02

Use-case identification

Identifying where AI can meaningfully support project delivery — manual, repetitive, or data-heavy work where automation adds real value, not applying it for its own sake.

03

Workflow integration

Connecting AI and automation into the tools and data already in use — including data pipeline and ETL work to centralize fragmented information across systems like Primavera P6, Microsoft Project, Power BI, and Power Apps — without disrupting how teams already work.

04

Data and governance requirements

Establishing the data quality, access, and governance foundation any AI use case depends on, before implementation begins.

05

Pilot implementation

Testing a use case in a controlled, real environment before wider rollout — moving a working proof of concept forward and validating it against real data and real operating conditions.

06

Responsible AI adoption

Governance, data quality, and team readiness treated as first-class requirements alongside the technology itself — AI applied only where it demonstrably strengthens existing project-controls processes.

07

Team enablement

Helping teams build confidence and capability working alongside new AI-driven tools and recommendations, so adoption sticks after delivery rather than going unused. Practical, tool-specific training is part of the broader engagement, not specific to AI adoption.

08

Scaling successful AI use cases

Extending a validated use case to more teams, more projects, or more of the organization once it's proven — the same targeted, proportionate approach used at every earlier stage.

Responsible by design

Intelligence expressed through structure, not hype

AI is applied where it demonstrably strengthens existing project-controls processes — never as a headline feature. Governance, data quality, and team readiness are treated as first-class requirements alongside the technology itself.
